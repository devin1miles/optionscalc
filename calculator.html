Greeks Reference

Δ
Delta — Price Sensitivity
Rate of change in option price per $1 move in the underlying. Delta 0.40 = +$0.40 per $1 rise.
CALL: 0 to +1PUT: -1 to 0
ATM ≈ ±0.50. Also approximates probability of expiring ITM.
θ
Theta — Time Decay
Dollar amount the option loses per day as expiration approaches.
LONG: costs dailySHORT: earns daily
Accelerates in final 30 DTE. Credit sellers benefit every day.
ν
Vega — Volatility Sensitivity
Dollar change per 1% shift in IV. Vega 0.08 = +$8/contract if IV rises 1%.
LONG: IV up = profitSHORT: IV up = loss
Selling into high IV = core edge of credit spread strategies.
Γ
Gamma — Delta Acceleration
Rate of change of delta per $1 move. High gamma = delta shifts fast.
LONG: positiveSHORT: negative
Spikes near expiry for ATM options. Dangerous for credit sellers.
ρ
Rho — Rate Sensitivity
Change per 1% shift in risk-free rate. Smallest Greek for short-dated options.
CALL: positivePUT: negative
Matters most for LEAPS. Minimal effect at 30–60 DTE.
IVR
IV Rank — Volatility Context
Where current IV sits vs its 52-week range. IVR 80 = top 20% of history.
HIGH >50: sellLOW <30: buy
High IVR = inflated premium = favorable for credit sellers.
